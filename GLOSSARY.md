# Guide to Hidden Gems

A collection of editor-written restaurant guides that helps diners find places that fit what they're looking for.

## Language

### Guides

**Guide**:
An editor-written, themed collection of restaurants with a title, description, tags and cover photo.
_Avoid_: List, collection, article

**Guide Index**:
The browsable, searchable set of all Guides, each shown as a summary.
_Avoid_: Home, guide list, catalogue

**Guide Item**:
One Restaurant's appearance in a Guide: the editor's write-up and photos of that Restaurant for that Guide. The same Restaurant can appear in several Guides as different Guide Items.
_Avoid_: Entry, pick, listing, guide restaurant

**Tag**:
A keyword an editor attaches to a Guide to say what it is about.
_Avoid_: Label, keyword, category (Categories belong to Restaurants)

### Restaurants

**Restaurant**:
A place to eat, with its own name, branch, rating, address, contact details, Categories and Working Hours, independent of any Guide.
_Avoid_: Shop, store, venue, place

**Category**:
A kind of food or venue that classifies a Restaurant, such as "ร้านกาแฟ/ชา" (coffee/tea shop).
_Avoid_: Tag, cuisine, type

**Rating**:
A Restaurant's average star score from 0 to 5, always read together with its number of reviews.
_Avoid_: Score, stars

**Unrated Restaurant**:
A Restaurant with no reviews yet. Its Rating means "unknown", not "zero stars".
_Avoid_: Zero-star restaurant

**Working Hours**:
The days of the week, and the opening and closing times, when a Restaurant is open. A day that isn't listed is a closed day.
_Avoid_: Opening times, schedule

**Open Now**:
A Restaurant whose Working Hours include the current moment in Thailand time, including hours that run past midnight from the previous day.
_Avoid_: Currently open, available

**Unavailable Restaurant**:
A Guide Item whose Restaurant details couldn't be loaded. The Guide Item still shows the editor's write-up and photos.
_Avoid_: Broken item, missing restaurant
